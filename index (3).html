      How scoring works
      • Each right answer scores 100, 200 or 300 by difficulty, plus a speed bonus. Your multiplier boosts both.
      • Get answers right in a row to raise your multiplier: x2 at 3, x3 at 6, x4 at 10.
      • Right answers add 2 seconds (4 for the big hands-on rounds). Wrong answers cost no time, but they reset your streak and multiplier.
      • No spamming: a wrong answer in under 1.5 seconds is a snap guess and costs 3 seconds.
      • Questions get harder the more you get right.
